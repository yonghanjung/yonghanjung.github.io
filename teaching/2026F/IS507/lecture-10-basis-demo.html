Every choice is the same method: pick pieces φ1, …, φK, then choose the weights β by least squares on the training points. The rule stays linear in β, yet bends in x. The ReLU pieces max(0, x − ck) use fixed, evenly spaced bends; a neural network would also learn where to bend. With enough pieces every family can follow the training points; switch on the test points to see when it stops helping.
